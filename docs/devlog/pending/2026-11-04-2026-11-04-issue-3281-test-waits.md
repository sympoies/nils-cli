## 2026-11-04 - Shorten agent-session unit-test waits
<!-- devlog-id: 2026-11-04-2026-11-04-issue-3281-test-waits -->

### Result

- agent-session unit tests use a 20 ms post-paste settle delay and a 2.5 s hold
  budget instead of 500 ms and 4 s; production values are unchanged.
- The child-process wait in run_output_with_timeout polls from 0.5 ms backing
  off to 10 ms instead of a fixed 10 ms; the timeout, kill and error paths are
  unchanged.

### Why / context

- Fake-tmux fixtures launched hundreds of short commands per test, and each
  paid a fixed wait.

### Evidence

- Same 2156 unit tests through the gate wrapper: summed time 1634 s before,
  1450 s after.
