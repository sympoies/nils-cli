## 2026-11-04 - Shorten agent-session unit-test waits
<!-- devlog-id: 2026-11-04-2026-11-04-issue-3281-test-waits -->

### Result

- agent-session unit tests use a 20 ms post-paste settle delay and a 2.5 s hold
  budget instead of 500 ms and 4 s; production values are unchanged.
- The swallowed-Enter prompt-receipt test uses a 40 ms fake transport delay
  instead of 120 ms, so it no longer races the 15 s guarded deadline on slow
  runners.

### Why / context

- Fake-tmux fixtures launched hundreds of short commands per test, and each
  paid a fixed wait.

### Evidence

- An adaptive child-wait poll was tried and dropped: it failed
  `uncertain_wrapped_rendering_never_submits` in 4 of 30 paired runs where the
  base failed 0 of 30.
